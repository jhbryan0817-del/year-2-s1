# CIVL2108 past-paper workflow tracker

## How consistent are the questions?

The **20 downloaded papers** contain a strong recurring core, with changes in syllabus emphasis over time. The largest groups are procurement-route reasoning (13 question rows in 11 papers), CPM calculations (12 rows in 12 papers), progress monitoring/coordination (12 rows in 12 papers), and project roles (12 rows in 12 papers). Repeated calculation patterns also include residual site valuation, IRR and material/labour/plant unit rates. Numerical inputs and project settings often change while the method remains recognisable.

Recent coverage matters: **all five available papers from December 2022 to December 2024 contain CPM and discounted-cash-flow appraisal**, while circular-economy questions occur in three of those five. Earlier papers put more emphasis on residual valuation, tender-error procedures and professional ethics. For example, the CPM activity table in 2018 December Q2 is reused in 2021 December Q4, while 2024 May Q2 adds a changed-network technology decision. Learn the transferable steps and practise the variants rather than memorising a previous answer.

The tracker contains **162 question/workflow rows covering all 95 numbered questions** in the 20 papers. Related subparts within one numbered question share a row when they use the same workflow; distinct workflows are separated. Thus row totals measure practice opportunities, and the separate paper count indicates how widely a pattern occurs. Groups are sorted by row count, largest first; ties are alphabetical. Rows within each group are most recent first. Every status starts as **Not solved**.
