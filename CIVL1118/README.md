# CIVL1118 — Structural Mechanics

This course studies how deformable structures respond to load: the links between internal force, stress, strain and deformation, and the limits set by **strength, stiffness and stability**. The first lecture gives this overview on **PDF pages 6–7**, with the teaching schedule on **page 4**. See [Lecture 1: Introduction](lecture-slides/01-introduction.pdf#page=4), downloaded from the [current Moodle resource](https://moodle.hku.hk/mod/resource/view.php?id=4187292).

The current [Semester 1 Moodle course](https://moodle.hku.hk/course/view.php?id=145332) is taught by Prof. H. Ye and Prof. J.J. Wang. Assessment is **60% final examination, 30% assignments and 10% practical/laboratory work** (first lecture, PDF page 3). The laboratory concerns buckling of struts. The [formal course description](lecture-slides/course-description.pdf#page=2) expands the learning outcomes and syllabus.

## Course content and where to start

The topic sequence below follows the first lecture's teaching schedule (PDF page 4), the current Moodle lecture headings, and the course outline (PDF pages 2–4).

| Lecture | Content | Main question-first practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [1](lecture-slides/01-introduction.pdf) | Structural idealisation, supports, loads, free-body diagrams, equilibrium, internal-force signs, assumptions, strength/stiffness/stability | Establish a clean model and check reactions before doing calculations. |
| [2](lecture-slides/02-axial-loading.pdf) | Axial stress/strain, elastic deformation, compatibility and thermal effects | Axial-member movement and restrained/dissimilar bars. |
| [3](lecture-slides/03-bending-internal-force.pdf) | Reactions and internal force/moment in statically determinate beams and frames | Beam/frame AFD, SFD and BMD, including hinges and inclined members. |
| [4](lecture-slides/04-bending-stress.pdf) | Section properties, parallel-axis theorem and symmetric bending stress | Locate centroid/I, identify critical moment, calculate stress or size a section. |
| [5](lecture-slides/05-normal-stress-advanced-topics.pdf) | Advanced normal stress, unsymmetric and composite sections | Neutral axis and material stiffness compatibility; normal stress under combined loading. |
| [6](lecture-slides/06-shear-stress-basic-topics.pdf) | Basic beam shear stress | V, Q, I and local thickness; critical stress locations. |
| [7](lecture-slides/07-shear-stress-advanced-topics.pdf) | Advanced shear stress and shear centre | Shear flow, built-up sections/connectors and shear-centre reasoning. |
| [8](lecture-slides/08-torsion.pdf) | Torsion of circular shafts and thin-walled tubes | Torque equilibrium, shear stress/strain, twist and compatibility. |
| [9](lecture-slides/09-stress-transformation.pdf) | Two-dimensional stress/strain transformation and Mohr's circle | Principal stresses, maximum shear and rotated-plane directions. |
| [10](lecture-slides/10-strength-theory.pdf) | Strength/failure criteria | State the required criterion and check governing points using Tresca/von Mises. |
| [11](lecture-slides/11-column-buckling.pdf) | Column stability and Euler buckling | Effective length, axis-dependent restraints, load sharing and critical mode. |
| [12](lecture-slides/12-deflection.pdf) | Beam flexural deformation | Elastic curve, superposition, rotations and deformation compatibility. |
